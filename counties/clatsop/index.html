About Clatsop County

Clatsop County () is the northernmost county in the U.S. state of Oregon. As of the 2020 census, the population was 41,072. The county seat is Astoria. The county is named for the Clatsop tribe of Native Americans, who lived along the coast of the Pacific Ocean prior to European settlement. Clatsop County comprises the Astoria, OR Micropolitan Statistical Area, or Sunset Empire, and is located in Northwest Oregon.

Clatsop County: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the county seat of Clatsop County, Oregon?

Astoria is the county seat of Clatsop County.

What cities are in Clatsop County, Oregon?

Clatsop County has 5 incorporated cities: Astoria, Seaside, Warrenton, Gearhart, Cannon Beach.

What is the population of Clatsop County, Oregon?

Clatsop County had 41,072 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census. The largest city is Astoria.

When was Clatsop County established?

Clatsop County was established in 1844, formed from northern and western portions of the original Twality District.

How did Clatsop County get its name?

Named for the Clatsop, a local Native American tribe.
