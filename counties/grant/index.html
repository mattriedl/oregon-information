About Grant County

Grant County is one of the 36 counties in the U.S. state of Oregon. As of the 2020 census, the population was 7,233, making it Oregon's fourth-least populous county. The county seat is Canyon City. It is named for President Ulysses S. Grant, who served as an army officer in the Oregon Territory, and at the time of the county's creation was a Union general in the American Civil War. Grant County is included in the eight-county definition of Eastern Oregon.

Grant County: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the county seat of Grant County, Oregon?

Canyon City is the county seat of Grant County.

What cities are in Grant County, Oregon?

Grant County has 10 incorporated cities: John Day, Prairie City, Canyon City, Mount Vernon, Long Creek, Seneca, Dayville, Monument, Granite, Greenhorn.

What is the population of Grant County, Oregon?

Grant County had 7,233 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census. The largest city is John Day.

When was Grant County established?

Grant County was established in 1864, formed from parts of old Wasco and old Umatilla counties.

How did Grant County get its name?

Named for Ulysses S. Grant prior to his election as president, in recognition of his military service.
