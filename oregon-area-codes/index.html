Oregon Area Codes

Which area code covers which part of Oregon: 503, 971, 541 and 458.

Home › Oregon Area Codes

Oregon has four area codes serving two regions. 503 and its overlay 971 cover the Portland metro area, Salem, the north coast and the northern Willamette Valley. 541 and its overlay 458 cover the rest of the state, including Eugene, Corvallis, the central and south coast, Southern Oregon, Central Oregon and Eastern Oregon. Because both regions have overlays, all local calls in Oregon require ten-digit dialing (area code plus number).

Area Codes 503 and 971

Counties: Clackamas, Clatsop, Columbia, Marion, Multnomah, Polk, Tillamook, Washington, Yamhill.

Major cities: Portland, Salem, Gresham, Hillsboro, Beaverton, Tigard, Lake Oswego, Keizer, Oregon City, McMinnville, Woodburn, Astoria, Tillamook, Seaside, St. Helens.

Area Codes 541 and 458

Counties: Baker, Benton, Coos, Crook, Curry, Deschutes, Douglas, Gilliam, Grant, Harney, Hood River, Jackson, Jefferson, Josephine, Klamath, Lake, Lane, Lincoln, Linn, Malheur, Morrow, Sherman, Umatilla, Union, Wallowa, Wasco, Wheeler.

Major cities: Eugene, Bend, Medford, Springfield, Corvallis, Albany, Redmond, Grants Pass, Roseburg, Klamath Falls, Pendleton, Hermiston, Coos Bay, Newport, Hood River, The Dalles, Ontario, Ashland.

Area code boundaries follow telephone exchanges, not county lines, so a few communities near the edges are exceptions. Cell phone numbers keep the area code where they were issued.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the area codes in Oregon?

Oregon has four area codes: 503 and 971 (Portland, Salem and northwest Oregon) and 541 and 458 (the rest of the state).

What area code is Portland, Oregon?

Portland uses area codes 503 and 971.

What area code is Bend, Oregon?

Bend uses area codes 541 and 458, like the rest of Central Oregon.

What area code is Eugene, Oregon?

Eugene uses area codes 541 and 458.

What area code is Salem, Oregon?

Salem uses area codes 503 and 971.

Do I need to dial the area code for local calls in Oregon?

Yes. Because 971 overlays 503 and 458 overlays 541, every local call in Oregon requires ten-digit dialing.
