About Newport

Newport is an incorporated city in Lincoln County, in Oregon’s Central Coast region, with a 2020 Census population of 10,256.

Newport is a city in and the county seat of Lincoln County, Oregon, United States. It was incorporated in 1882 with the name dating back to the establishment of a post office in 1868. Newport was named for Newport, Rhode Island. As of the 2020 census, the city had a total population of 10,256.

It is also home of the Oregon Coast Aquarium, Hatfield Marine Science Center, Nye Beach, Yaquina Head Light, Yaquina Bay Light, Newport Sea Lion Docks, Pacific Maritime Heritage Center, and the former Rogue Ales. The city is the western terminus of U.S. Route 20, a cross-country highway that originates in Boston and is the longest road in the United States.

Newport, Oregon: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Newport, Oregon?

Newport had 10,256 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 9,989 in 2010).

What county is Newport, Oregon in?

Newport is in Lincoln County, in the Oregon Coast region of Oregon. It is the county seat of Lincoln County.

How far is Newport from Portland?

Newport is about 90 miles from Portland in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Newport, Oregon?

The closest incorporated city is Toledo, about 6 miles away. Other nearby cities include Siletz, Depoe Bay, Waldport.

When was Newport, Oregon incorporated?

Newport was incorporated as a city in 1882.

What is the cost of living in Newport, Oregon?

Oregon has no statewide sales tax, but it does have a state income tax (4.75% to 9.9%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Newport is in the Oregon Coast region. See our Oregon cost of living guide for typical housing, utility and grocery costs.

What school district serves Newport, Oregon?

Newport is served by Lincoln County School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Newport?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Central Lincoln PUD (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Newport.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Newport?

Within 30 days of becoming an Oregon resident, you need both Oregon vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.
