About Phoenix

Phoenix is an incorporated city in Jackson County, in Oregon’s Rogue Valley region, with a 2020 Census population of 4,475.

Phoenix is a city in Jackson County, Oregon, United States. The population was 4,475 at the 2020 census. Phoenix is a part of the Medford Metropolitan Statistical Area, in the Rogue Valley, and is 3 miles (5 km) southeast of Medford on Interstate 5.

Phoenix, Oregon: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Phoenix, Oregon?

Phoenix had 4,475 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (4,538 in 2010).

How big is Phoenix, Oregon?

Phoenix covers about 1.43 square miles of land and had 4,475 residents at the 2020 Census, a density of about 3,129 people per square mile.

What county is Phoenix, Oregon in?

Phoenix is in Jackson County, in the Southern Oregon region of Oregon. The county seat is Medford.

How far is Phoenix from Portland?

Phoenix is about 224 miles from Portland in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Phoenix, Oregon?

The closest incorporated city is Talent, about 3 miles away. Other nearby cities include Medford, Jacksonville, Ashland.

What is the ZIP code for Phoenix, Oregon?

Phoenix's ZIP code is 97535. Some addresses near the city limits use a neighboring town's ZIP code, so check a specific address with USPS.

When was Phoenix, Oregon incorporated?

Phoenix was incorporated as a city in 1910.

What is the cost of living in Phoenix, Oregon?

Oregon has no statewide sales tax, but it does have a state income tax (4.75% to 9.9%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Phoenix is in the Southern Oregon region. See our Oregon cost of living guide for typical housing, utility and grocery costs.

What school district serves Phoenix, Oregon?

Phoenix is served by Phoenix-Talent SD 4. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Phoenix?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Pacific Power (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Phoenix.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Phoenix?

Within 30 days of becoming an Oregon resident, you need both Oregon vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.
