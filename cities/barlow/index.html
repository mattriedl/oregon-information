About Barlow

Barlow is an incorporated city in Clackamas County, in Oregon’s Portland Metro region, with a 2020 Census population of 133.

Barlow is a city in Clackamas County, Oregon, United States. The population was 133 at the 2020 census. The mayor of Barlow is Robert Lang Larson.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Barlow, Oregon?

Barlow had 133 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (135 in 2010).

What is the cost of living in Barlow, Oregon?

Barlow lies in the Portland Metro region, where costs vary mainly by housing. Oregon charges no statewide sales tax.

What utilities do I set up when moving to Barlow?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Portland General Electric (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer, and trash are typically arranged through the City of Barlow.

How do I find a job near Barlow?

WorkSource Oregon offers free career coaching, and iMatchSkills is the state's largest job board.

Which schools serve families in Barlow?

Assignment depends on your exact address. Start with the Oregon Department of Education directory or the Clackamas County district office.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Barlow?

Within 30 days of establishing residency — both the vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.

Are there food banks or assistance programs near Barlow?

Yes. Apply for SNAP at the ONE Oregon portal, find a pantry through Oregon Food Bank, or dial 211 for Clackamas County community resources.
