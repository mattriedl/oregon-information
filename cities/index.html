List of Cities in Oregon (A–Z)

All 241 incorporated cities in Oregon, with county and 2020 Census population.

Home › Oregon Cities

Oregon has 241 incorporated cities spread across its 36 counties. Together they are home to about 3.0 million people — about 71% of the state’s 4.24 million residents (2020 Census). Portland is the largest city, Salem is the state capital, and Greenhorn is the smallest, with 3 residents at the 2020 Census. Every city below links to its own guide with population history, schools, utilities, weather, nearby cities and official links. You can also browse cities by county or by region.
