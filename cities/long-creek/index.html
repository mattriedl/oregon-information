About Long Creek

Long Creek is an incorporated city in Grant County, in Oregon’s Eastern Oregon region, with a 2020 Census population of 173.

Long Creek is a city in Grant County, Oregon, United States. The population was 173 at the 2020 census. The city is named after John Long, a prominent miner who came to Grant County in 1862 during the Canyon City rush of that same year. Located in a high valley of the Blue Mountains, ranching has been the principal enterprise for the area. Log harvesting was slowly curtailed in the nearby Malheur National Forest until the local mill was closed in 1998.

Long Creek, Oregon: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Long Creek, Oregon?

Long Creek had 173 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (197 in 2010).

How big is Long Creek, Oregon?

Long Creek covers about 1.02 square miles of land and had 173 residents at the 2020 Census, a density of about 170 people per square mile.

What county is Long Creek, Oregon in?

Long Creek is in Grant County, in the Eastern Oregon region of Oregon. The county seat is Canyon City.

How far is Long Creek from Portland?

Long Creek is about 183 miles from Portland in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Long Creek, Oregon?

The closest incorporated city is Monument, about 17 miles away. Other nearby cities include Mount Vernon, John Day, Canyon City.

What is the ZIP code for Long Creek, Oregon?

Long Creek's ZIP code is 97856. Some addresses near the city limits use a neighboring town's ZIP code, so check a specific address with USPS.

When was Long Creek, Oregon incorporated?

Long Creek was incorporated as a city in 1891.

What is the cost of living in Long Creek, Oregon?

Oregon has no statewide sales tax, but it does have a state income tax (4.75% to 9.9%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Long Creek is in the Eastern Oregon region. See our Oregon cost of living guide for typical housing, utility and grocery costs.

What school district serves Long Creek, Oregon?

Long Creek is served by Long Creek SD 17. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Long Creek?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Oregon Trail Electric Cooperative (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Long Creek.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Long Creek?

Within 30 days of becoming an Oregon resident, you need both Oregon vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.
