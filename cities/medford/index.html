About Medford

Medford is an incorporated city in Jackson County, in Oregon’s Rogue Valley region, with a 2020 Census population of 85,824.

Medford is a city in and the county seat of Jackson County, Oregon, in the United States. As of the 2020 United States census the city had a total population of 85,824, making it the eighth-most populous city in Oregon, and a metropolitan area population of 223,259, making the Medford Metropolitan Statistical Area the fifth largest metro area in Oregon. The city was named in 1883 by David Loring, civil engineer and right-of-way agent for the Oregon and California Railroad, after Medford, Massachusetts, which was near Loring's hometown of Concord, Massachusetts. Medford is near the middle fork of Bear Creek.

Medford, Oregon: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Medford, Oregon?

Medford had 85,824 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 74,907 in 2010).

What county is Medford, Oregon in?

Medford is in Jackson County, in the Southern Oregon region of Oregon. It is the county seat of Jackson County.

How far is Medford from Portland?

Medford is about 219 miles from Portland in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Medford, Oregon?

The closest incorporated city is Central Point, about 2 miles away. Other nearby cities include Jacksonville, Phoenix, Eagle Point.

When was Medford, Oregon incorporated?

Medford was incorporated as a city in 1885.

What is the cost of living in Medford, Oregon?

Oregon has no statewide sales tax, but it does have a state income tax (4.75% to 9.9%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Medford is in the Southern Oregon region. See our Oregon cost of living guide for typical housing, utility and grocery costs.

What school district serves Medford, Oregon?

Medford is served by Medford School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Medford?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Pacific Power (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Medford.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Medford?

Within 30 days of becoming an Oregon resident, you need both Oregon vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.
