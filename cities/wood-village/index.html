About Wood Village

Wood Village is an incorporated city in Multnomah County, in Oregon’s Portland Metro region, with a 2020 Census population of 4,387.

Wood Village is a city in Multnomah County, Oregon, United States. It is located between Fairview to the west, Troutdale to the east, and Gresham to the south. The population was 4,387 at the 2020 census. Despite the name, Wood Village is classified as a city.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Wood Village, Oregon?

Wood Village had 4,387 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 3,878 in 2010).

What is the cost of living in Wood Village, Oregon?

Wood Village lies in the Portland Metro region, where costs vary mainly by housing. Oregon charges no statewide sales tax.

What utilities do I set up when moving to Wood Village?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Portland General Electric (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer, and trash are typically arranged through the City of Wood Village.

How do I find a job near Wood Village?

WorkSource Oregon offers free career coaching, and iMatchSkills is the state's largest job board.

Which schools serve families in Wood Village?

Assignment depends on your exact address. Start with the Oregon Department of Education directory or the Multnomah County district office.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Wood Village?

Within 30 days of establishing residency — both the vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.

Are there food banks or assistance programs near Wood Village?

Yes. Apply for SNAP at the ONE Oregon portal, find a pantry through Oregon Food Bank, or dial 211 for Multnomah County community resources.
