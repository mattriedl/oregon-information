About Troutdale

Troutdale is an incorporated city in Multnomah County, in Oregon’s Portland Metro region, with a 2020 Census population of 16,300.

Troutdale is a city in Multnomah County, Oregon, United States, immediately north of Gresham and east of Wood Village. As of the 2020 census, the city population was 16,300. The city serves as the western gateway to the Historic Columbia River Highway, the Mount Hood Scenic Byway, and the Columbia River Gorge. It is approximately 12 miles (19 km) east of Portland and is part of the Portland, Oregon metropolitan area.

Troutdale, Oregon: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Troutdale, Oregon?

Troutdale had 16,300 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 15,962 in 2010).

How big is Troutdale, Oregon?

Troutdale covers about 5.95 square miles of land and had 16,300 residents at the 2020 Census, a density of about 2,739 people per square mile. By population it is Oregon's 37th-largest city out of 241.

What county is Troutdale, Oregon in?

Troutdale is in Multnomah County, in the Portland Metro region of Oregon. The county seat is Portland.

How far is Troutdale from Portland?

Troutdale is about 14 miles from Portland in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Troutdale, Oregon?

The closest incorporated city is Wood Village, about 1 miles away. Other nearby cities include Fairview, Gresham, Maywood Park.

What is the ZIP code for Troutdale, Oregon?

Troutdale's ZIP code is 97060. Some addresses near the city limits use a neighboring town's ZIP code, so check a specific address with USPS.

When was Troutdale, Oregon incorporated?

Troutdale was incorporated as a city in 1907.

What is the cost of living in Troutdale, Oregon?

Oregon has no statewide sales tax, but it does have a state income tax (4.75% to 9.9%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Troutdale is in the Portland Metro region. See our Oregon cost of living guide for typical housing, utility and grocery costs.

What school district serves Troutdale, Oregon?

Troutdale is served by Reynolds School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Troutdale?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Portland General Electric (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Troutdale.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Troutdale?

Within 30 days of becoming an Oregon resident, you need both Oregon vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.
