About Sandy

Sandy is an incorporated city in Clackamas County, in Oregon’s Portland Metro region, with a 2020 Census population of 12,612.

Sandy is a city located in Clackamas County, Oregon, United States, settled c. 1853 and named after the nearby Sandy River. Located in the foothills of the Cascade Mountain Range, the city serves as the western gateway to the Mount Hood Corridor, and is located approximately 27 miles (43 km) east of Portland.

The city of Sandy was originally settled by travelers passing along Barlow Road, one of the final sections of the Oregon Trail, and initially known as Revenue, after settlers Francis and Lydia Revenue. The city subsequently took the name Sandy after the Sandy River, named by Meriwether Lewis and William Clark in 1805; the river and previously been named the Barings River, after Sir Francis Baring, 1st Baronet, following a 1792 expedition in the region.

Sandy, Oregon: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Sandy, Oregon?

Sandy had 12,612 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 9,570 in 2010).

How big is Sandy, Oregon?

Sandy covers about 3.57 square miles of land and had 12,612 residents at the 2020 Census, a density of about 3,533 people per square mile. By population it is Oregon's 43rd-largest city out of 241.

What county is Sandy, Oregon in?

Sandy is in Clackamas County, in the Portland Metro region of Oregon. The county seat is Oregon City.

How far is Sandy from Portland?

Sandy is about 22 miles from Portland in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Sandy, Oregon?

The closest incorporated city is Estacada, about 8 miles away. Other nearby cities include Troutdale, Gresham, Happy Valley.

What is the ZIP code for Sandy, Oregon?

Sandy's ZIP code is 97055. Some addresses near the city limits use a neighboring town's ZIP code, so check a specific address with USPS.

When was Sandy, Oregon incorporated?

Sandy was incorporated as a city in 1911.

What is the cost of living in Sandy, Oregon?

Oregon has no statewide sales tax, but it does have a state income tax (4.75% to 9.9%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Sandy is in the Portland Metro region. See our Oregon cost of living guide for typical housing, utility and grocery costs.

What school district serves Sandy, Oregon?

Sandy is served by Sandy School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Sandy?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Portland General Electric (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Sandy.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Sandy?

Within 30 days of becoming an Oregon resident, you need both Oregon vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.
