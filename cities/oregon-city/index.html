About Oregon City

Oregon City is an incorporated city in Clackamas County, in Oregon’s Portland Metro region, with a 2020 Census population of 37,572.

Oregon City is a city in and the county seat of Clackamas County, Oregon, United States, located on the Willamette River near the southern limits of the Portland metropolitan area. As of the 2020 census, the city population was 37,572. Established in 1829 by the Hudson's Bay Company, in 1844, it became the first U.S. city west of the Rocky Mountains to be incorporated.

Oregon City, Oregon: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Oregon City, Oregon?

Oregon City had 37,572 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 31,859 in 2010).

How big is Oregon City, Oregon?

Oregon City covers about 10.05 square miles of land and had 37,572 residents at the 2020 Census, a density of about 3,739 people per square mile. By population it is Oregon's 16th-largest city out of 241.

What county is Oregon City, Oregon in?

Oregon City is in Clackamas County, in the Portland Metro region of Oregon. It is the county seat of Clackamas County.

How far is Oregon City from Portland?

Oregon City is about 12 miles from Portland in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Oregon City, Oregon?

The closest incorporated city is Gladstone, about 2 miles away. Other nearby cities include West Linn, Johnson City, Lake Oswego.

What is the ZIP code for Oregon City, Oregon?

Oregon City's ZIP code is 97045. Some addresses near the city limits use a neighboring town's ZIP code, so check a specific address with USPS.

When was Oregon City, Oregon incorporated?

Oregon City was incorporated as a city in 1844.

What is the cost of living in Oregon City, Oregon?

Oregon has no statewide sales tax, but it does have a state income tax (4.75% to 9.9%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Oregon City is in the Portland Metro region. See our Oregon cost of living guide for typical housing, utility and grocery costs.

What school district serves Oregon City, Oregon?

Oregon City is served by Oregon City School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Oregon City?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Portland General Electric (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Oregon City.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Oregon City?

Within 30 days of becoming an Oregon resident, you need both Oregon vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.
