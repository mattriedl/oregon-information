About Springfield

Springfield is an incorporated city in Lane County, in Oregon’s Willamette Valley region, with a 2020 Census population of 61,851.

Springfield is a city in Lane County, Oregon, United States. Located in the Southern Willamette Valley, it is within the Eugene-Springfield metropolitan statistical area. Separated from Eugene to the west, mainly by Interstate 5, Springfield is the second-most populous city in the metropolitan area after Eugene. As of the 2020 census, the city has a total population of 61,851, making it the ninth-most populous city in Oregon.

The Briggs family first settled the Springfield area, arriving in 1848. The community was incorporated as a city in 1885. The city was named after a natural spring located in a field or prairie within the current city boundaries. For the majority of the 20th century, the economy of Springfield was largely dependent on the Oregon timber industry; since the 1990s, however, the economy has diversified, with PeaceHealth now the largest employer in the city. Public education in the city is provided by the Springfield School District.

Springfield, Oregon: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Springfield, Oregon?

Springfield had 61,851 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 59,403 in 2010).

What county is Springfield, Oregon in?

Springfield is in Lane County, in the Willamette Valley region of Oregon. The county seat is Eugene.

How far is Springfield from Portland?

Springfield is about 103 miles from Portland in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Springfield, Oregon?

The closest incorporated city is Eugene, about 5 miles away. Other nearby cities include Coburg, Creswell, Lowell.

When was Springfield, Oregon incorporated?

Springfield was incorporated as a city in 1885.

What is the cost of living in Springfield, Oregon?

Oregon has no statewide sales tax, but it does have a state income tax (4.75% to 9.9%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Springfield is in the Willamette Valley region. See our Oregon cost of living guide for typical housing, utility and grocery costs.

What school district serves Springfield, Oregon?

Springfield is served by Springfield School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Springfield?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by EWEB / Pacific Power (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Springfield.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Springfield?

Within 30 days of becoming an Oregon resident, you need both Oregon vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.
