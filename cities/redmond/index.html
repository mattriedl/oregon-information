About Redmond

Redmond is an incorporated city in Deschutes County, in Oregon’s Central Oregon region, with a 2020 Census population of 33,274.

Redmond is a city in Deschutes County, Oregon, United States. The population was 33,274 at the 2020 census, and according to 2023 census estimates, the city is estimated to have a population of 37,009.

The city is on the eastern side of Oregon's Cascade Range, in the High Desert in Central Oregon. From Redmond there is access to recreational opportunities. Redmond is a full-service municipality and one of the fastest-growing industrial and residential communities in Oregon.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Redmond, Oregon?

Redmond had 33,274 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 26,215 in 2010).

What is the cost of living in Redmond, Oregon?

Redmond lies in the Central Oregon region, where costs vary mainly by housing. Oregon charges no statewide sales tax.

How do I connect utilities in Redmond?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Pacific Power / Central Electric Cooperative (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer, and trash are typically arranged through the City of Redmond.

How do I find a job near Redmond?

WorkSource Oregon offers free career coaching, and iMatchSkills is the state's largest job board.

Which schools serve families in Redmond?

Assignment depends on your exact address. Start with the Oregon Department of Education directory or the Deschutes County district office.

After moving to Redmond, what's the DMV deadline?

Within 30 days of establishing residency — both the vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.

Are there food banks or assistance programs near Redmond?

Yes. Apply for SNAP at the ONE Oregon portal, find a pantry through Oregon Food Bank, or dial 211 for Deschutes County community resources.
