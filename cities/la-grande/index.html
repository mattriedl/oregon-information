About La Grande

La Grande is an incorporated city in Union County, in Oregon’s Northeast Oregon region, with a 2020 Census population of 13,026.

La Grande () is a city in and the county seat of Union County, Oregon, United States. La Grande is Union County's largest city, with a population of 13,026 at the time of the 2020 U.S. Census. The La Grande micropolitan population is 25,076. It is the 16th largest metro or micropolitan area in Oregon.

La Grande is located on the western margin of the Grande Ronde Valley that is bounded by the Blue Mountains and Wallowa Mountains. These mountains and the Grande Ronde River offer abundance of natural resources and wildlife. Initially a logging and agriculture town, it has become a sports and recreation destination.

It is the hub for surrounding communities offering outdoor recreation, shopping centers and entertainment. La Grande is known for its theater and arts as the downtown area boasts Liberty Theater and the Elgin Opera House often has local showings. La Grande also is a college town with a student population of 2,825 at Eastern Oregon University.

La Grande, Oregon: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of La Grande, Oregon?

La Grande had 13,026 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (13,082 in 2010).

How big is La Grande, Oregon?

La Grande covers about 4.58 square miles of land and had 13,026 residents at the 2020 Census, a density of about 2,844 people per square mile. By population it is Oregon's 41st-largest city out of 241.

What county is La Grande, Oregon in?

La Grande is in Union County, in the Eastern Oregon region of Oregon. It is the county seat of Union County.

How far is La Grande from Portland?

La Grande is about 223 miles from Portland in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near La Grande, Oregon?

The closest incorporated city is Island City, about 2 miles away. Other nearby cities include Imbler, Summerville, Union.

What is the ZIP code for La Grande, Oregon?

La Grande's ZIP code is 97850. Some addresses near the city limits use a neighboring town's ZIP code, so check a specific address with USPS.

When was La Grande, Oregon incorporated?

La Grande was incorporated as a city in 1865.

What is the cost of living in La Grande, Oregon?

Oregon has no statewide sales tax, but it does have a state income tax (4.75% to 9.9%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. La Grande is in the Eastern Oregon region. See our Oregon cost of living guide for typical housing, utility and grocery costs.

What school district serves La Grande, Oregon?

La Grande is served by La Grande School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to La Grande?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Oregon Trail Electric Cooperative (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of La Grande.

How soon must I register my car after moving to La Grande?

Within 30 days of becoming an Oregon resident, you need both Oregon vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.
