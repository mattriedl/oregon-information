Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gearhart, Oregon affordable to live in?

Gearhart lies in the North Coast region, where costs vary mainly by housing. Oregon charges no statewide sales tax.

What utilities do I set up when moving to Gearhart?

Electricity is generally provided by Portland General Electric; natural gas, where available, by NW Natural. Water, sewer, and trash are handled by the City of Gearhart.

What is the job market like around Gearhart?

WorkSource Oregon offers free career coaching, and iMatchSkills is the state's largest job board.

Which schools serve families in Gearhart?

Assignment depends on your exact address. Start with the Oregon Department of Education directory or the Clatsop County district office.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Gearhart?

Within 30 days of establishing residency — both the vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.

Are there food banks or assistance programs near Gearhart?

Yes. Apply for SNAP at the ONE Oregon portal, find a pantry through Oregon Food Bank, or dial 211 for Clatsop County community resources.
