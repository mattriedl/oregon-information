Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Garibaldi, Oregon?

Garibaldi had 830 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 779 in 2010).

Is Garibaldi, Oregon affordable to live in?

Garibaldi lies in the North Coast region, where costs vary mainly by housing. Oregon charges no statewide sales tax.

What utilities do I set up when moving to Garibaldi?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Tillamook PUD (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer, and trash are typically arranged through the City of Garibaldi.

What is the job market like around Garibaldi?

WorkSource Oregon offers free career coaching, and iMatchSkills is the state's largest job board.

Which schools serve families in Garibaldi?

Assignment depends on your exact address. Start with the Oregon Department of Education directory or the Tillamook County district office.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Garibaldi?

Within 30 days of establishing residency — both the vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.

Are there food banks or assistance programs near Garibaldi?

Yes. Apply for SNAP at the ONE Oregon portal, find a pantry through Oregon Food Bank, or dial 211 for Tillamook County community resources.
