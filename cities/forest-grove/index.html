About Forest Grove

Forest Grove is an incorporated city in Washington County, in Oregon’s Portland Metro region, with a 2020 Census population of 26,225.

Forest Grove is a city in Washington County, Oregon, United States, 25 miles (40 km) west of Portland, within the Tualatin Valley. Originally a small farm town, it is now primarily a commuter town in the Portland metro area. Settled in the 1840s, the town was platted in 1850, then incorporated in 1872, making it the first city in Washington County.

The population was 26,225 at the 2020 census, an increase of 8% over 2010 figure (21,083).

Pacific University is located here. Old College Hall on campus, and nine other sites in the city, are listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

Forest Grove, Oregon: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Forest Grove, Oregon?

Forest Grove had 26,225 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 21,083 in 2010).

What county is Forest Grove, Oregon in?

Forest Grove is in Washington County, in the Portland Metro region of Oregon. The county seat is Hillsboro.

How far is Forest Grove from Portland?

Forest Grove is about 21 miles from Portland in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Forest Grove, Oregon?

The closest incorporated city is Cornelius, about 3 miles away. Other nearby cities include Hillsboro, Gaston, Banks.

When was Forest Grove, Oregon incorporated?

Forest Grove was incorporated as a city in 1872.

What is the cost of living in Forest Grove, Oregon?

Oregon has no statewide sales tax, but it does have a state income tax (4.75% to 9.9%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Forest Grove is in the Portland Metro region. See our Oregon cost of living guide for typical housing, utility and grocery costs.

What school district serves Forest Grove, Oregon?

Forest Grove is served by Forest Grove School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Forest Grove?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Portland General Electric (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Forest Grove.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Forest Grove?

Within 30 days of becoming an Oregon resident, you need both Oregon vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.
