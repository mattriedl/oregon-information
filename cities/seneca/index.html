About Seneca

Seneca is an incorporated city in Grant County, in Oregon’s Eastern Oregon region, with a 2020 Census population of 165.

Seneca is a city in Grant County, Oregon, United States. It is located in the Blue Mountains about 23 miles (37 km) south of Canyon City, on U.S. Route 395, on the edge of the Malheur National Forest. As of the 2020 census, Seneca had a population of 165.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Seneca, Oregon?

Seneca had 165 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (199 in 2010).

What is the cost of living in Seneca, Oregon?

Seneca lies in the Eastern Oregon region, where costs vary mainly by housing. Oregon charges no statewide sales tax.

What utilities do I set up when moving to Seneca?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Oregon Trail Electric Cooperative (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer, and trash are typically arranged through the City of Seneca.

How do I find a job near Seneca?

WorkSource Oregon offers free career coaching, and iMatchSkills is the state's largest job board.

Which schools serve families in Seneca?

Assignment depends on your exact address. Start with the Oregon Department of Education directory or the Grant County district office.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Seneca?

Within 30 days of establishing residency — both the vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.

Are there food banks or assistance programs near Seneca?

Yes. Apply for SNAP at the ONE Oregon portal, find a pantry through Oregon Food Bank, or dial 211 for Grant County community resources.
