About Fairview

Fairview is an incorporated city in Multnomah County, in Oregon’s Portland Metro region, with a 2020 Census population of 10,424.

Fairview is a city in Multnomah County, Oregon, United States. It is located in the Portland metropolitan area north of Gresham and west of Troutdale. The population was 10,424 at the 2020 census.

Fairview, Oregon: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Fairview, Oregon?

Fairview had 10,424 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 8,920 in 2010).

How big is Fairview, Oregon?

Fairview covers about 3.09 square miles of land and had 10,424 residents at the 2020 Census, a density of about 3,373 people per square mile. By population it is Oregon's 50th-largest city out of 241.

What county is Fairview, Oregon in?

Fairview is in Multnomah County, in the Portland Metro region of Oregon. The county seat is Portland.

How far is Fairview from Portland?

Fairview is about 12 miles from Portland in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Fairview, Oregon?

The closest incorporated city is Wood Village, about 1 miles away. Other nearby cities include Troutdale, Gresham, Maywood Park.

What is the ZIP code for Fairview, Oregon?

Fairview's ZIP code is 97024. Some addresses near the city limits use a neighboring town's ZIP code, so check a specific address with USPS.

When was Fairview, Oregon incorporated?

Fairview was incorporated as a city in 1908.

What is the cost of living in Fairview, Oregon?

Oregon has no statewide sales tax, but it does have a state income tax (4.75% to 9.9%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Fairview is in the Portland Metro region. See our Oregon cost of living guide for typical housing, utility and grocery costs.

What school district serves Fairview, Oregon?

Fairview is served by Reynolds School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Fairview?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Portland General Electric (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by NW Natural, Avista or Cascade Natural Gas. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Fairview.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Fairview?

Within 30 days of becoming an Oregon resident, you need both Oregon vehicle registration and an Oregon driver's license.
