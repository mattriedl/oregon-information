Oregon Information for Residents, Visitors and Newcomers

Oregon Information is a free, independent guide to the State of Oregon. Every one of Oregon's 241 incorporated cities has its own page with 2020 Census population, county, local schools, utilities, weather and links to official city websites. You can also browse all 36 Oregon counties and the state's seven regions, from the Oregon Coast and the Willamette Valley to Central Oregon, Southern Oregon, Eastern Oregon, the Columbia River Gorge and the Portland metro area.

Thinking about relocating? Our moving to Oregon guide covers cost of living, housing, taxes and DMV deadlines. Planning a trip? The Visit Oregon guide covers top destinations, road trips and outdoor activities, and the Oregon Information blog adds in-depth articles on living in the Beaver State.
