Oregon Quick Facts: Frequently Asked Questions

More state facts, symbols and history are on our Oregon state guide.

What is the capital of Oregon?

Salem is the capital of Oregon. Portland is the state's largest city.

How many cities and counties are in Oregon?

Oregon has 241 incorporated cities and 36 counties, grouped on this site into seven regions: Portland Metro, the Willamette Valley, the Oregon Coast, the Columbia River Gorge, and Central, Eastern and Southern Oregon.

What is the population of Oregon?

Oregon had 4,237,256 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census, and current estimates put the population above 4.2 million.

Does Oregon have a sales tax?

No. Oregon is one of five states with no general statewide sales tax. It does have a state income tax, with rates from 4.75% to 9.9%.

When did Oregon become a state?

Oregon became the 33rd U.S. state on February 14, 1859. Its nickname is the Beaver State.
